## 🧹 PRIORITY 3: Repository Hygiene & Curation (30 Minutes)

A cluttered GitHub with abandoned repos hurts credibility. Treat your GitHub like an art gallery.

### 3.1 Audit All Public Repositories
Visit `https://github.com/v0786?tab=repositories`:
- **Archive or Make Private**:
  - Repositories created just to test a single command.
  - Incomplete school projects from years ago that don't reflect your current React/TypeScript skills.
  - Forked repos with 0 commits of your own.
  - *How to make private*: Go to Repo &rarr; Settings &rarr; Danger Zone &rarr; "Change repository visibility" &rarr; "Make private".
  - *How to archive*: Settings &rarr; Danger Zone &rarr; "Archive this repository" (marks it as read-only).

### 3.2 Ensure Every Public Repo Has:
1. A **Descriptive Title** in kebab-case or clean CamelCase.
2. A **1-sentence About description** and **relevant topic tags**.
3. A **Live Demo link** (GitHub Pages, Vercel, or Netlify).
4. A **Standardized README** (see Section 4 below).
