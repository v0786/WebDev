Layer 2: API Gateway Serverless
Call Dispatch /api/calls

Authenticates dispatch requests and proxies to n8n Webhook 07.

Post-Call Webhook /api/webhooks

Idempotently relays OmniDimension call results to n8n Webhook 08.

Layer 3: n8n Engine 14 Workflows
Intake & Dedup WF 01-05

Normalizes E.164 (+91), categorizes websites, checks DNC & deduplicates.

Queue & Call WF 06-12

Rate-limits calling queue (10 AM-7 PM IST) & processes post-call AI classifications.

Layer 4: AI Telephony & DB Workers
OmniDimension AI Voice Agent

AI Voice telephony engine placing outbound calls to business owners.

Supabase DB PostgreSQL

Persistent database store (`leads`, `calls`, `call_results`, `followups`).
